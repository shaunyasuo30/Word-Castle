# Gameplay sound recordings

The files here are edited from CC0 recordings published on Freesound:

| Local file | Original recording |
| --- | --- |
| `cannon_fire_1.mp3` | [Cannon fire; shells fall nearby](https://freesound.org/people/craigsmith/sounds/675611/) by craigsmith. Vintage practical film effect. |
| `cannon_fire_2.mp3` | [Cannon Fire](https://freesound.org/people/raphavpires/sounds/867028/) by raphavpires. Foley recorded from physical objects. |
| `bomb_blast.mp3` | [Heavy sharp explosion with reverb](https://freesound.org/people/craigsmith/sounds/674896/) by craigsmith. Vintage practical film effect. |
| `firework_burst.mp3` | [blow.aif](https://freesound.org/people/Heigh-hoo/sounds/21410/) by Heigh-hoo. A single field-recorded firework burst. |
| `go.mp3`, `victory.mp3`, `defeat.mp3` | [Surfer dude — generic lines kit](https://freesound.org/people/Sadiquecat/sounds/818978/) by Sadiquecat. Human voice actor. |

The high-quality MP3 previews were trimmed to individual cues, normalized and faded at the tail. All source pages list the [CC0 1.0 license](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). The game layers the bomb and firework recordings when a word explodes.
